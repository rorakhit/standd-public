## The first goal in club history

You remember where you are when history happens. Gillette, 72nd minute — Gutierres plays this inch-perfect forward pass that slips right between Utah's last two defenders. But Traoré doesn't break away clean. She's got two defenders on her back. She uses her strength to muscle her way through both of them and slots it home.

First goal in Boston Legacy history. Right there. She earned every inch of it.

The stadium roared — what a moment to experience. The scoreline was already 2-0 and the result was already written, but honestly? None of that dulled it.

## How Utah pulled away

Utah struck first on a set piece in the 33rd minute — a well-worked free kick routine, Lacasse delivering to the back post, Milazzo finishing cleanly. Good goal. Frustrating to concede.

We had some really good build-up play in that first half though, and we were unlucky not to get a penalty of our own. The attacking intent was there — we just need to find our scoring boots.

The one that really stings came early in the second half: another penalty. That's the second consecutive match we've given one up, and we'd already had St-Georges see red against Gotham earlier in the season. I'm not hitting the panic button yet, but I'd be lying if I said the discipline stuff isn't starting to make me a little nervous. Three matches in and we're already picking up yellows, a red, and two penalties conceded. That's a pattern worth watching.

Prašnikar put the penalty into the top left corner. 2-0. And then Traoré pulled one back and gave us something to actually feel good about.

## The honest take

Boston had 60% possession and 11 shots to Utah's 7. We're not being outplayed in every area — far from it. But the margins in this league are so fine that a set piece and a penalty was enough to decide it.

This is a growing team learning what it takes to compete at this level. The pieces are there. It just hasn't clicked yet — and right now every small mistake costs us more than it should. The goal gives everyone something to build on. The other stuff is homework.
