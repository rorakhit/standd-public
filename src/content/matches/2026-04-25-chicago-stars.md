## The numbers tell an uncomfortable story

27 shots. 9 corners. 8 on target.

Final score: Chicago 2, Boston 0.

You can dominate a match statistically and still lose by two. It happens. But when it keeps happening — and it has — you have to start asking hard questions about where the goals are actually going to come from.

## How Chicago scored

First goal came in the 10th minute off a corner. The ball deflected off one of our players toward Huitema in the six-yard box and she tapped it in. Second came in the 51st minute on a 2-on-1 breakaway — Gareis released Gomes, who slipped it past Murphy. Both were moments of transition or set pieces. Chicago didn't sustain pressure. They just took their chances when they got them.

Atkinson made eight saves to earn her first NWSL clean sheet of the season. The two back-to-back stops just before halftime were the real turning point — if either of those goes in, the second half looks completely different.

## What we brought

We dominated the game between the boxes. Gutierres orchestrating, Traoré as the focal point, Prince and Hasbo providing width. We moved the ball, created openings, kept them in their own half for long stretches.

A 43rd-minute Traoré drive forced a strong save. A 48th-minute shot went wide. Stevens, Najjemba, and Olivieri all came on at 60 looking for something. The attacking pressure kept coming. The goal never did.

## The pattern

Five matches in and we've scored once — Traoré's goal against Utah. We're not sitting back. We're generating chances, getting shots off, putting pressure on. That's something.

But the finishing isn't there yet. And until it is, nights like this one are going to keep happening. Atkinson was excellent on Saturday. We also need to be more ruthless when those chances come. Those two things are both true.

## The honest take

0-5-0 is not a crisis for a brand new club in their first NWSL season. It is, honestly, exactly what this looks like when the league is competitive and the roster is still finding itself. Chicago are not a great team. We should be beating them. We will beat them eventually.

Wednesday is North Carolina at Gillette. They're a good team and it won't be easy. But that's the thing about caring about a club — you show up anyway.
